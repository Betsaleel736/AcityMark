  1. Lecturer starts a session. A QR code appears with a countdown.
  2. Students scan it. Their phone asks to share its location once.
  3. The server decides. Code valid, not expired, enrolled, within range, not already marked.

What the location check can and cannot do

It stops someone marking attendance from a hostel room or from home. It cannot prove exactly where a person stands inside a building, and a determined student can fake a GPS reading. Treat it as a first filter, and use the lecturer's attendance list to review anything unusual.
